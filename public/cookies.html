Cookie Policy

Last Updated: January 15, 2025

1. Introduction

Welcome to MedGenie – Care at Your Fingertips. This Cookie Policy explains how MedGenie uses cookies and similar tracking technologies on our website and applications. By using MedGenie, you consent to the use of cookies as described in this policy.

2. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, smartphone, or tablet) when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies help us:

  • Remember your preferences and settings
  • Understand how you use our service
  • Improve your experience on our platform
  • Keep you logged in securely
  • Analyze usage patterns and optimize performance

3. Types of Cookies We Use

Essential Cookies

These cookies are necessary for the website to function properly. They enable core functionality such as security, network management, and accessibility. You cannot opt out of these cookies as they are essential for using MedGenie.

  • Authentication and session management
  • Security and fraud prevention
  • Load balancing and performance optimization

Performance Cookies

These cookies help us understand how visitors interact with MedGenie by collecting and reporting information anonymously. This helps us improve our service.

  • Analytics and usage statistics
  • Error tracking and debugging
  • Page load times and performance metrics

Functionality Cookies

These cookies allow MedGenie to remember choices you make (such as your preferred language or region) and provide enhanced, more personalized features.

  • User preferences and settings
  • Language and location preferences
  • Customized content delivery

Targeting/Advertising Cookies

These cookies may be set through our site by our advertising partners. They may be used to build a profile of your interests and show you relevant content on other sites.

  • Personalized content recommendations
  • Marketing and promotional communications
  • Interest-based advertising (where applicable)

4. How We Use Cookies

MedGenie uses cookies for the following purposes:

  • Authentication: To keep you logged in securely and verify your identity
  • Security: To protect against fraud and unauthorized access
  • Preferences: To remember your settings and customize your experience
  • Analytics: To understand how users interact with our platform and improve our services
  • Performance: To optimize loading times and ensure smooth functionality
  • Feature Development: To test new features and improve existing ones

5. Third-Party Cookies

In addition to our own cookies, we may use third-party services that set cookies on your device. These services include:

  • Analytics Services: Google Analytics, Mixpanel, or similar tools to analyze usage patterns
  • Authentication Providers: OAuth providers for social login functionality
  • Content Delivery Networks (CDNs): To deliver content efficiently
  • Payment Processors: For secure payment processing (if applicable)

These third parties have their own privacy and cookie policies. We recommend reviewing their policies to understand how they use cookies.

6. Managing Cookies

You have several options for managing cookies:

Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can typically:

  • View which cookies are stored on your device
  • Delete existing cookies
  • Block cookies from being set
  • Set preferences for specific websites

Please note that blocking or deleting cookies may affect your experience on MedGenie and limit certain functionality.

Browser-Specific Instructions

  • Chrome: Settings → Privacy and security → Cookies and other site data
  • Firefox: Options → Privacy & Security → Cookies and Site Data
  • Safari: Preferences → Privacy → Manage Website Data
  • Edge: Settings → Privacy, search, and services → Cookies and site permissions

7. Your Choices

You can choose to accept or decline cookies. However, please be aware that:

  • Declining essential cookies may prevent you from accessing certain features of MedGenie
  • Some functionality may not work properly without cookies enabled
  • You may need to log in more frequently if you disable authentication cookies
  • Personalization features will be limited without functionality cookies

You can also opt out of interest-based advertising by visiting:

  • Your Online Choices (EU)
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (US)
  • Digital Advertising Alliance of Canada
